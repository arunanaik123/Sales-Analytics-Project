# Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level overview of the company's sales performance and helps senior management monitor key business metrics, identify trends, and support strategic decision-making.

---

# KPI 1 - Total Revenue

## Business Question
What is the total revenue generated by the company during the analysis period?

## Business Value
Measures the company's overall sales performance and serves as the primary indicator of business growth.

## Tables Used
- Order_Details

## SQL Concepts Used
- SUM()

## Recommended Visualization
KPI Card
